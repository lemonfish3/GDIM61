# ⚔️ Convalescence

**Convalescence** is a 2D action-RPG for **PC**, where players uncover the secrets of the Fallen God — the bringer of discord — and fight to restore balance to a fractured world.

## 🎮 Game Overview
In Convalescence, players master a variety of combat strategies while battling through hordes of enemies. As the story unfolds, you will unravel the mystery behind the Fallen God and bring harmony back to the world once and for all.

## 🕹️ Features
- 2D action-RPG gameplay
- Fast-paced combat with strategic depth
- Narrative-driven world and lore
- Challenging enemy encounters
